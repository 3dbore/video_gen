# Spec: NEWS-01: PROMISE, rover Bulan dari suku cadang rover Mars
Source: house style (the brief sets `reference: none`)

## Pacing
House spec, adapted to a 55s vertical reel for Instagram watched on a phone. The brief's eight beats are the scenes (6, 7, 8, 7, 6, 6, 7, 8s), so the narration sets the scene lengths and nothing is cut faster than the voice. Inside a beat, one new idea lands every 1.5 to 2.5s, timed to the word that names it, which gives about 4 to 6 landings per 10s. This works because a phone viewer reads one thing at a time: each landing gives the eye a new target just as the ear hears it.

The pace speeds up in Beat 5, where four labels land on four spoken parts in 6s, about one every 1.5s, the fastest stretch of the film. It slows at Beat 7, the hero: "Akhir 2028" lands and holds longest of anything in the film, at least 3s, before "18 bulan" enters. Beat 8 is the stillest scene, closing on the question and the logo.

## First motion
House spec: something moves within 0.3s and the point is visible by 1.5s. Here the isometric screen panel starts dropping onto the white grid by 0.3s, and the title "Rover Bulan dari suku cadang Mars." is readable by 1.5s, so the scroller gets motion first and the claim before they decide to swipe. The NASA clip starts playing inside the panel on the first spoken word, as the brief asks.

## Cut rhythm
Hard cuts only, and only on the 7 beat changes. Each cut also changes the field, following the brief and DESIGN-VIDEO.md fields by scene: Beat 1 white, Beat 2 cobalt, Beat 3 white, Beat 4 navy, Beat 5 cobalt, Beat 6 white, Beat 7 navy, Beat 8 chartreuse. A field change makes each cut read instantly, even on a small screen. Between cuts, change comes from objects dropping, wires drawing and labels popping, never from new shots. The rhythm tightens in Beat 5 and stops in Beat 7, where the hero number holds longest.

## Look
See design.md. Palette, type, radius, spacing and logo come from it, using the brief's five colours: #001848, #3065EF, #DBEB42, #FB4988, #FFFFFF.

Video style: A (Isometric system), per brand/DESIGN-VIDEO.md. Reason: the brief asks for Style A for the whole film, and the script explains how parts connect (Mars rover parts wired to PROMISE, a generator, four instruments, two test technologies), with several values read as labels. That matches the section 4 rows "shows several parts that work together" and tie-breaker 1 (three or more related objects).

Layout habits: 1080 x 1920 canvas, no text or logo in the top 220 px or bottom 380 px. Isometric objects with 3 px navy outlines and hard 10 px navy shadows, no gradients or blur. Chartreuse only on the element that moves or matters. Iso labels are navy pills with a chartreuse title and white detail on a navy stem with a chartreuse dot. The copy block sits around y 1180 with the "SCENE 0X · NAME" eyebrow in Label style. NASA clips and the photo sit inside an isometric screen panel, muted, with the credit line under them in Caption style. One moving element per scene.

## Sound
No music, as the brief says, and effects are CC0 only and subtle. The voice carries the film. Effects land only where something lands: a soft tap when the Beat 1 panel lands, soft clicks on labels and boxes, short ticks on parts arriving and on each Beat 5 label, one short low hum on the generator pulse, and one short rising sound on the logo. At the key moment, the ticks speed up as "Akhir 2028" comes in and stop dead when it lands, so the silence holds the date.

- The Beat 1 title is readable by 1.5s.
- Exactly 7 hard cuts, on the beat changes, and the field colour changes at every one, in the order white, cobalt, white, navy, cobalt, white, navy, chartreuse.
- One moving element at a time in every scene, and every object has a navy outline and a hard shadow, with no gradient or blur.
- No text or logo inside the top 220 px or bottom 380 px in any still.
- In Beat 5, each label lands within 0.3s of its spoken word.
- "Akhir 2028" holds still longer than any other element before a change, at least 3s before "18 bulan" enters, and stays the largest text in Beat 7.
- Beat 8 is the stillest scene: nothing moves for the last 1.2s or more after the logo lands.
