# Story: NOS-02: Lengan robot open source dari motor servo murah

## One point
Lengan robot open source ini memakai motor servo biasa yang harganya puluhan ribu rupiah di toko online Indonesia.

## Hero number
Rp22.500 sampai Rp71.745
This is what one MG90S servo costs across the listings we sampled, and it turns "a robot arm" into something a student can picture buying. It lands in Beat 4 at about 29s, once the viewer has heard the free design and the three parts and has just been asked "Berapa harga motornya?"

## Beat 1: Tension (0-22.2s)
The viewer assumes a robot arm is out of reach. The first second shows an arm on navy under the title "Lengan robot dari motor servo murah", and the joint bends on "gratis". The design is free under the MIT licence, with its maker credited, and the arm comes down to three parts: motor servo, papan driver, Arduino. That leaves one open question: what does the motor cost?
Maps to brief beats: 1, 2, 3

## Beat 2: Proof (22.2-37.9s)
"Berapa harga motornya?" The number climbs to Rp22.500, then on to Rp71.745, with ticks speeding up. The ticks stop dead as the full range lands on a chartreuse plate, the biggest thing in the film, and its echo stack collapses to one plate. Then the stronger servo enters beneath it, smaller: Rp53.350 sampai Rp78.480. A small line says these are sample prices from Indonesian online stores, checked 7 Nov 2026, and differ by store.
VO: "Berapa harga motornya? Satu servo MG90S, sekitar dua puluh dua ribu sampai tujuh puluh dua ribu rupiah. Servo MG996R yang lebih kuat, sekitar lima puluh tiga sampai tujuh puluh delapan ribu."
Maps to brief beats: 4

## Beat 3: So what (37.9-50s)
The film stays honest first: this is only the motor price. The number of motors and the power supply are still being checked, so no total appears. For a student or new maker, the first step is now clear and affordable, and the printed parts are the next question. The last image is the still cobalt card with the 3D BORE logo, "Mau print part-nya? Tanya kami." and "WhatsApp atau DM Instagram @3dbore".
Maps to brief beats: 5, 6

## Changes from the brief
All were asked for by the person on 7 Nov 2026, after their price screenshots disagreed with the brief's figures. The brief itself stays frozen.
- **Hero number:** "Rp34 ribu" becomes the range "Rp22.500 sampai Rp71.745". Its source listing now shows Rp71.745, so the single price no longer holds.
- **MG996R:** "Rp58 ribu sampai Rp87 ribu" becomes "Rp53.350 sampai Rp78.480", leaving out three listings marked 360 degrees because a continuous-rotation servo can't hold a joint angle.
- **Beat 4 VO:** the spoken prices change to match, as written above. The weight moves to the MG90S range.
- **Beat 4 motion:** a count-up can't stop on a range, so the number climbs to the low price, then on to the high price, before the full range lands.
- **Beat 2 VO:** "dengan lisensi MIT" is cut from the narration because the voice kept mispronouncing it. The MIT licence stays on screen, as the brief requires.
- **Length:** 45s becomes 50s, following the voice, which runs 47.68s. The person agreed on 7 Nov 2026.
- **Check date:** 6 Nov 2026 becomes 7 Nov 2026 everywhere, the date of the screenshots.
- **Beat 4 source line:** becomes "Contoh harga toko online Indonesia, dicek 7 Nov 2026. Beda tiap toko." because this is a sample from one results screen.
- **Beat 6 credit:** "Harga: Tokopedia, 6 Nov 2026" becomes "Harga: toko online Indonesia, 7 Nov 2026". The person doesn't want to promote or mention any stores, so the film names no store and no marketplace.
