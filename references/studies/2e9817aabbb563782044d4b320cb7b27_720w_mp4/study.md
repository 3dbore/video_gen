# Study: 2e9817aabbb563782044d4b320cb7b27_720w.mp4

720x720, 30 fps, 4.8s, with sound effects (no beat found, 31 onsets). No speech. A short kinetic type ad. All frames read, every 0.5s, plus 10 fps frames from 0.5 to 1.7s.

## 1. Pacing
6 shots, cuts at 0.533, 1.667, 2.733, 3.667, 4.333. Median shot 0.80s, shortest 0.47s. 10.4 cuts per 10s. Evenly fast throughout. Too short and too fast to pace a 45s narrated film.

## 2. First motion
On screen by 0.04s (first sound onset 0.04, first picture change window 0 to 0.3s). "Think" is already moving when the viewer arrives.

## 3. Cut rhythm
Mostly type swaps on a constant light field. Only the last cut, at 4.333 (change value 229), flips the field to black for "(it happens)".

## 4. Text behavior
- "FAST" lands on a solid colour plate and trails copies of itself.
- "Can't find your iPhone?" mixes huge and small words in the same frame, and the huge words get cropped by the frame edge.
- A typing cursor appears on "Can".

## 5. Easing feel
Hard landing, then a slow release. Judged from 10 fps frames: the plate arrives at about 0.5s, its echo stack grows to about 6 copies by about 0.8s, then loses copies until one plate remains at about 1.5s. `measure curve` on this window gave rmse values from 0.7 to 14, so its ease names mean nothing here and were discarded.

## 6. Payoff
"(it happens)" at 4.333s: small white text on black after all the loud type. The device is the sudden drop in scale and colour.

## 7. Rules to copy
- A landing word sits on a solid plate in one accent colour.
- The plate trails echo copies that build within about 0.3s and collapse to one within about 0.7s (judged).
- The landing word is much larger than anything else in that frame.

## 8. Things NOT to copy
Its green, its wording, the product reference, the italic bold type and the crop-off-the-frame words where they'd hurt legibility.
