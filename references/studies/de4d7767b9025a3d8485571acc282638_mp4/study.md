# Study: de4d7767b9025a3d8485571acc282638.mp4

720x480, 60 fps, 8.38s, no audio. A logo animation for a performing arts venue: the wordmark opens a window that expands into footage and collapses back. All frames read, every 0.5s.

## 1. Pacing
10 cuts, 11 shots. Median 0.75s, shortest 0.30s. 11.9 cuts per 10s. Calm to 2.1s, a burst of 0.30s shots from 2.483 to 3.383, then slowing again (1.12, 1.57s) to the logo. Several cuts are window content changes rather than full-frame cuts.

## 2. First motion
About 0.73s (`measure events` hold of 0.73s at the start). The logo sits still first, then its centre block starts to grow, so the viewer reads the name before anything happens.

## 3. Cut rhythm
Hard cuts inside the window, on a burst from 2.483 to 3.383. The window itself pushes open to full frame and closes again.

## 4. Text behavior
The wordmark is the only type. Its two halves are pushed apart by the window between them, and they close up again at the end.

## 5. Easing feel
Smooth and confident, no overshoot (judged). Not measured, because moving footage defeats `measure curve`.

## 6. Payoff
About 8s: the wordmark closes up to its original form. The device is a return to the opening frame.

## 7. Rules to copy
- Start and end on the same logo frame.
- A burst of very short shots only works when it sits between calm stretches.

## 8. Things NOT to copy
The footage, the venue's identity, its wordmark, and pulling a logo apart.
